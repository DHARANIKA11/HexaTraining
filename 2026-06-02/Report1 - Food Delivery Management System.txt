Food Delivery Management System

 Assessment Report

1. Project Overview

The Food Delivery Management System is designed to manage customers, restaurants, delivery partners, and food orders for an online food delivery platform. The database enables efficient storage, retrieval, and analysis of order transactions and customer activities.

The project demonstrates the use of MongoDB concepts including:

* Document-Oriented Database Design
* Collections and Documents
* Embedded Documents and Arrays
* CRUD Operations (Insert, Find, Update, Delete)
* Query Operators

Stores customer information such as customer ID, name, city, membership type, and contact details.

 Project Objectives

* Manage customer and restaurant information efficiently.
* Track food orders and delivery operations.
* Analyze revenue generated through different payment modes.
* Monitor restaurant and delivery partner performance.
* Generate business reports for decision-making.
* Perform advanced analytics using aggregation and lookup operations.
